How we work

Six stages, each signed off by you before the next one starts.

  1. Site visit

    Our engineer visits your plot, checks soil and access, and listens to what you need. Free within Islamabad and Rawalpindi.

  2. Drawings and BOQ

    You get plans, a 3D elevation and an itemised bill of quantities. The BOQ price is the price you pay.

  3. Approvals

    We submit drawings to the relevant authority and handle every query until the approval letter is issued.

  4. Grey structure

    Foundations to roof slab, with a site engineer present for every concrete pour.

  5. Finishing

    Materials are shown to you and approved before they are fixed. Nothing is substituted without your sign-off.

  6. Handover

    Final walkthrough, snag list cleared, completion certificate and a 10-year structural warranty.
